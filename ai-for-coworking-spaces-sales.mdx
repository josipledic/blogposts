# How AI Can Help Coworking Spaces Convert Tour Requests Into Suitable Memberships

Customers notice delays and mistakes, not the technology behind them. A sensible AI project for coworking spaces therefore starts with a recurring service problem.

## The operational gap

A freelancer seeking two quiet days a week needs a different conversation from a team planning to grow.

A guided inquiry can collect work pattern, call needs, access hours and growth plans, then suggest relevant options for staff to discuss. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

Prices and availability come from live systems. The assistant should explain differences, not push the most expensive plan.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with recent tours and the memberships customers eventually chose. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
