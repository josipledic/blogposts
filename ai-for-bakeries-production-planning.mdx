# How AI Can Help Bakeries Plan Daily Production With Less Waste

Customers notice delays and mistakes, not the technology behind them. A sensible AI project for bakeries therefore starts with a recurring service problem.

## The operational gap

Friday demand is not the same after a holiday, during rain or when a nearby school closes.

A modest forecast can combine till history, advance orders and known local events to suggest starting quantities by product. The gain comes from preparation, not from letting software make the final call.

## Keep judgment in the business

The baker adjusts for weather, quality and local knowledge. Forecast error should be visible so the team learns when not to trust it.

Good automation is deliberately narrow. It knows which records it may use, what it may draft and exactly where human responsibility begins.

## Start with evidence

I would start with one product family with reliable sales and waste figures. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. Run the test beside the existing process before replacing anything. The team can then compare speed and quality with evidence rather than enthusiasm.
