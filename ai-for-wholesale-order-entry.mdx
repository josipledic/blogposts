# How AI Can Help Wholesalers Turn Messy Orders Into Clean Drafts

Customers notice delays and mistakes, not the technology behind them. A sensible AI project for wholesalers therefore starts with a recurring service problem.

## The operational gap

Regular customers still order by PDF, email and photographs of handwritten stock sheets.

AI can map their descriptions to catalog numbers, highlight uncertain matches and create a draft order in the ERP. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

A person confirms quantities, units and substitutions before release. An uncertain product match must be visible rather than silently accepted.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with orders from three customers with different formats. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
