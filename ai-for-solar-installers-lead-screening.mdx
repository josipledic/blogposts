# How AI Can Help Solar Installers Qualify Solar Inquiries Before The Site Visit

Most small businesses already hold the information needed to improve service. The problem is getting it to the right person in time. That is where AI can be useful for solar installers.

## The operational gap

A postcode and annual electricity bill are not enough to know whether a site visit is worthwhile.

A guided intake can collect roof material, orientation, shading, meter photos and ownership details, then prepare the project file. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

Satellite images and customer answers are preliminary. A qualified installer verifies structure, electrical conditions and yield assumptions.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with the information your surveyor repeatedly requests. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
