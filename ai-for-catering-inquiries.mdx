# How AI Can Help Catering Companies Reply To Event Inquiries While They Are Still Warm

The case for AI in catering companies is practical: protect skilled time while making the customer experience more consistent. That requires a focused workflow rather than a general-purpose bot.

## The operational gap

A promising inquiry can sit unanswered while the team is cooking or serving another event. By evening, the client may already have asked three competitors.

An assistant can read the request, pull out guest count, venue, date and dietary needs, then draft a response using real menu and minimum-order data. The gain comes from preparation, not from letting software make the final call.

## Keep judgment in the business

Availability and final pricing must come from the booking calendar and a responsible employee, not from a language model’s guess.

Good automation is deliberately narrow. It knows which records it may use, what it may draft and exactly where human responsibility begins.

## Start with evidence

I would start with the last month of inquiry emails. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. Run the test beside the existing process before replacing anything. The team can then compare speed and quality with evidence rather than enthusiasm.
