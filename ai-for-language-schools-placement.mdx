# How AI Can Help Language Schools Improve Course Placement Before The First Lesson

Small teams cannot add an administrator every time demand grows. For language schools, carefully bounded AI can remove clerical friction without removing the people customers trust.

## The operational gap

A multiple-choice score alone may place a learner badly because speaking confidence and practical goals differ.

An assistant can organize a short writing sample, recorded response and schedule needs for a teacher to review. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

The teacher makes the placement decision. Accent, name or background must never become a proxy for ability.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with one course level and teacher-reviewed sample responses. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
