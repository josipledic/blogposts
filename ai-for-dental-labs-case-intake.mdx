# How AI Can Help Dental Laboratories Catch Incomplete Case Submissions Early

AI earns its place in dental laboratories only when it improves real work. Faster preparation, cleaner information and fewer forgotten requests are better goals than novelty.

## The operational gap

A case can reach production without a shade photo, material choice or clear due date, forcing calls when time is already short.

An intake assistant can compare each submission with the lab’s case requirements and request the exact missing item. The employee starts with a coherent case rather than hunting through several channels.

## Keep judgment in the business

It does not evaluate clinical suitability or redesign a prescription. Technicians and prescribing clinicians resolve those decisions.

A system that sounds confident is not necessarily reliable. Useful integration makes its evidence and limits visible to the employee responsible for the outcome.

## Start with evidence

I would start with one common restoration type and its required inputs. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. Success should appear in the work itself: fewer follow-up calls, faster handling or less manual correction. Usage statistics alone do not prove value.
