# How AI Can Help Florists Turn Wedding Consultations Into Clear Briefs

Useful automation begins with an ordinary working day, not a futuristic demo. In florists, the best opportunity is often the handoff between a customer request and the next practical action.

## The operational gap

Color references, venue rules, budgets and inspiration photos can become confused between consultation and proposal.

AI can organize confirmed choices by bouquet, table and venue area, then draft a recap that invites correction. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

Taste is not a database field, and flower availability changes. The florist makes the creative and seasonal decisions.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with one consultation type with a stable questionnaire. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
