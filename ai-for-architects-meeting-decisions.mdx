# How AI Can Help Small Architecture Studios Keep Design Decisions From Disappearing

Small teams cannot add an administrator every time demand grows. For small architecture studios, carefully bounded AI can remove clerical friction without removing the people customers trust.

## The operational gap

A client preference mentioned in a long meeting can vanish until it conflicts with a later drawing.

A meeting assistant can draft decisions, open questions and responsible parties, with timestamps back to the recording. Staff receive usable context instead of another unstructured message.

## Keep judgment in the business

It cannot decide that a vague comment is approval. The architect reviews the record and the client confirms consequential choices.

The technical test is simple: can staff see the source, correct the draft and take over immediately? If not, the workflow is too opaque for customer-facing work.

## Start with evidence

I would start with one recurring project meeting with participant consent. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. The pilot needs a baseline and a modest target. Time saved is useful only if errors, customer effort and staff corrections are measured too.
