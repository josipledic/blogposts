# How AI Can Help Recruitment Agencies Turn Interview Notes Into Useful Candidate Records

The case for AI in recruitment agencies is practical: protect skilled time while making the customer experience more consistent. That requires a focused workflow rather than a general-purpose bot.

## The operational gap

Good information disappears when consultants postpone CRM notes until the end of a call-heavy day.

With consent, transcription can produce a factual summary of availability, experience and preferences for the consultant to edit. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

The model must not score personality, infer protected traits or decide who advances. Hiring judgment needs evidence and accountable people.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with a single interview format and an agreed note template. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
