# How AI Can Help Small Law Firms Reduce Friction In New-Client Intake

Revenue rarely leaks from a small business in one dramatic event. It leaks through slow replies, incomplete notes and work that must be entered twice. With AI, small law firms can close one of those gaps.

## The operational gap

A prospective client may send a long story without the dates, jurisdiction or opposing party needed for a conflict check.

A secure intake assistant can request the missing facts, create a chronology and prepare a concise brief for the first lawyer review. This removes repetitive sorting while keeping approval where it belongs.

## Keep judgment in the business

No automated message should imply that representation exists or provide legal advice. Confidentiality and conflict procedures control the design.

This is process design as much as model choice. Permissions, source records, review and failure handling determine whether the tool helps on a difficult day.

## Start with evidence

I would start with closed matters converted into fictional test cases. That sample is enough to expose missing information, repeated effort and exceptions that need a person. A contained trial reveals awkward exceptions early. It also provides a clean decision about continuing, changing the scope or stopping.
